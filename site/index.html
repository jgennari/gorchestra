01 / THE WORKFLOW

One direction.
Many ways forward.

Complex projects rarely need one uninterrupted conversation. Split the work into focused sessions, let each agent make progress, then bring the results back together.

01

Set the direction

Start with the project and the outcome you want. Keep the conversation, files, and work history together.

02

Weave in specialists

Delegate parallel tasks to different agents. Choose the model and thinking depth that fit each piece of work.

03

Follow through

Watch live progress and tool activity, collect durable reports, and carry the next decision forward.

02 / BUILT FOR VISIBILITY

See the work.
Keep the thread.

Threave keeps every session legible while your agents work. Follow the activity that matters, inspect files and changes, and come back to an ordered history after a refresh or a break.

  • Messages, thinking, tool calls, and command output as they happen
  • Child sessions and reports tied to the work that started them
  • Workspace files, changes, and follow-up prompts in one place
